In September 2026, I received the **Peking University Presidential Scholarship for Doctoral Students** (北京大学博士研究生校长奖学金). The university established the scholarship to encourage doctoral students to pursue research. [Peking University Graduate School](https://grs.pku.edu.cn/jzgz/069cf238798b402b998ea156d34696f2.htm)

I am pursuing a Ph.D. in **Signal and Information Processing** at the School of Electronics, where my research focuses on how AI can be used in wireless communication systems.
